The WHO calls upon international funding bodies and multinational tech developers to invest directly in local research capacity and open-access localized validation benchmarks across the Global South.

### Why It Matters
Traditional bioethics and clinical trial oversight mechanisms were designed for static pharmaceuticals and medical devices, making them structurally ill-suited for adaptive, self-learning AI algorithms. The WHO's lifecycle governance guidelines set an authoritative regulatory baseline that will directly influence health ministries, clinical trial registries, and medical AI developers worldwide, ensuring patient safety without stifling life-saving biomedical innovation.
