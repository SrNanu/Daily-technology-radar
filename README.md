# Daily Technology & AI Radar

Automated daily intelligence on Tech & AI curated 24/7 by an autonomous agent running on a dedicated local server.

---

## 📌 About the Project

**Daily Technology & AI Radar** is an autonomous intelligence pipeline designed to monitor, synthesize, and analyze the most critical breakthroughs and developments across the technology and artificial intelligence landscapes. 

Every 24 hours, an autonomous AI analyst scans global developments, extracts key takeaways, contextualizes strategic implications ("Why It Matters"), and compiles structured daily briefings with verified source citations.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Global Scanning**: Autonomous search engines track frontier AI publications, regulatory bodies, enterprise releases, and cybersecurity bulletins.
2. **Synthesis & Deep Analysis**: Raw intelligence is vetted, distilled into key takeaways, and evaluated for long-term strategic impact.
3. **Automated Deployment**: Daily markdown reports and repository indexes are version-controlled and published directly to GitHub.
